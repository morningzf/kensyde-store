# KENSYDE Retail Header Design QA

## Visual target

- Reference: user-provided Stanley homepage screenshot.
- Direction: KENSYDE Quiet Carry expressed through a strong modern retail framework.
- Target: Stanley-inspired white retail navigation with a distinct KENSYDE horizontal brand lockup, functional search, full-bleed campaign hero, bold sans-serif typography, and direct shopping actions.

## Completed checks

- Homepage structure preserves Hero, Colors, Scenes, Details, Best Sellers, Philosophy, and Newsletter.
- Hero is now a full-width campaign photograph with direct conversion copy at the lower left.
- Header uses the official K symbol and KENSYDE wordmark as a horizontal brand lockup.
- The stacked tagline version is no longer compressed into the navigation.
- Header retains clear retail navigation, support access, cart, and functional product search.
- The former small color grid is now a large horizontal product gallery with three-card desktop rhythm, one-card mobile rhythm, direct product links, and working arrow controls.
- The color gallery was refined into a compact retail rail: its title, description, and controls now share one clear header; four products are visible at desktop widths; and the cards no longer create excessive empty space.
- Product imagery is scaled up inside consistent studio canvases, while each color receives a restrained accent strip and matching information band.
- Product names, lifestyle tags, and prices use a consistent aligned footer treatment.
- Color cards preserve the real six supplier product images on clean white studio canvases, with restrained KENSYDE color accents.
- The rejected automated transparent cutouts are no longer referenced by active storefront code because they damaged fine product edges and reflective highlights.
- Original product images are used throughout the storefront to preserve exact bottle silhouettes, metal lids, body highlights, and true colors.
- Typography now uses Archivo for the bold, comfortable retail tone requested by the user.
- Brand color names and lifestyle tags remain display-only.
- Existing SKU, prices, product images, cart, checkout, and admin behavior remain unchanged.
- Mobile capture confirms the horizontal brand lockup remains legible without crowding the cart and menu.
- The supplied KENSYDE stacked brand mark remains in the footer.
- `next build` completed successfully.
- `npm run lint` completed successfully.

## Validation

- Local browser capture completed at the active mobile viewport.
- Header logo, hero copy, actions, and the next-section preview fit without overlap.
- Final desktop color-gallery capture confirms balanced spacing, complete product cards, and no awkward partial blank card at the right edge.
- Responsive card widths and horizontal scrolling are covered by the implemented mobile breakpoints.
- Product imagery was restored to the high-quality original files and displayed on white canvases to keep the existing floor shadow visually unobtrusive.
- Future campaign photography can add people and broader color collections while preserving KENSYDE's own product shape and identity.

final result: passed
